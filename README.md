# AI-SAF — review channel

This repository exists for one purpose: to receive criticism of the **AI Strategic Autonomy
Framework** (AI-SAF) and keep it in public, citable, and attached to a version.

The framework itself, the self-assessment instrument and the Evidence Base are at
**https://ai-sovereignty.vercel.app**. No code lives here.

## The state of the framework

AI-SAF is published at **v0.7.1** — deliberately before it is finished. Part V of the
framework document, *Areas of genuine uncertainty*, lists what the author is not sure
about, including the parts a methods reviewer is most likely to attack.

Two things stand between v0.7.1 and v1.0:

1. Published weight-sensitivity analysis (Monte Carlo, PCA cross-checks). Described in
   §4.3 as a design commitment; not yet run.
2. **At least two assessments performed by someone other than the author**, landing within
   a band of the author's own.

The second is the reason this repository exists.

## What is useful to send

Open an issue. The templates cover the three kinds of critique that change a version:

- **Methodology** — a weight, an anchor, an axis tag, or an aggregation rule you think is
  wrong, with the evidence you would move it on.
- **Evidence Base** — a 2024–2026 incident the record is missing, with primary sources.
- **Independent assessment** — you scored an organisation or a state against the published
  anchors and want to compare it with the author's reading.

Anything else is welcome as a blank issue.

## What makes a critique land

Point at something dated and sourced. The Evidence Base is the referee: every scoring
choice in the framework is supposed to trace to something that actually happened, and a
disagreement backed by an incident or a study beats a disagreement backed by intuition.

Disagreement that survives is credited in the changelog. Disagreement that kills a claim
is worth more than agreement that confirms one.
